# Travel and Location Policy

## Indicators

Transactions from a new country or region may require additional review when they differ from the customer's normal geographic activity.

Potential indicators include:

- Transactions from countries not previously associated with the customer.
- Rapid changes between geographically distant locations.
- Foreign transactions combined with a new device or unusual transaction amounts.

## Context

Geographic changes alone do not prove fraud.

Check available customer context for:

- Travel notices.
- Customer statements describing planned travel.
- Family members or authorized card users living or traveling abroad.
- Previous legitimate activity in the same location.

Relevant contextual evidence may explain otherwise suspicious geographic activity.

## Escalation

Recommend escalation when unusual geographic activity cannot be explained by available customer context.

Stronger action may be appropriate when unusual location evidence occurs alongside other strong indicators of unauthorized activity.

Blocking a card or escalating a case requires human approval before the action is executed.
