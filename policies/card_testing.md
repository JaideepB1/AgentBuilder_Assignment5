# Card Testing Policy

## Indicators

Potential card testing indicators include:

- Many transactions occurring within a short period of time.
- Repeated low-value authorization attempts.
- Multiple declined transactions followed by successful transactions.
- Rapid transactions across several merchants.
- Activity that differs substantially from the customer's normal transaction frequency.

## Context

Transaction bursts or declines alone do not prove card testing.

Compare the activity with the customer's historical transaction behaviour and consider available customer context before treating the pattern as fraudulent.

## Escalation

Recommend escalation when card testing appears plausible but evidence is incomplete.

Recommend blocking a card when transaction behaviour strongly indicates unauthorized card testing or when supporting customer evidence confirms unauthorized activity.

Blocking a card or escalating a case requires human approval before the action is executed.
