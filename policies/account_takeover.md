# Account Takeover Policy

## Indicators

Potential account takeover indicators include:

- Transactions from a new or unusual device.
- Transactions from countries inconsistent with the customer's normal activity.
- Sudden bursts of transactions that differ from the customer's historical behaviour.
- Unexpected device registration or password changes.
- Customer statements denying transactions, travel, login activity, or device registration.

## Context

Suspicious behaviour should not automatically be classified as fraud.

Customer notes may provide legitimate explanations for unusual devices, locations, transaction amounts, or spending patterns. Relevant case notes, disputes, and prior cases should be considered before determining a disposition.

## Escalation

Recommend escalation when account takeover is plausible but the available evidence is not sufficient for a confident fraud determination.

Recommend blocking a card only when the evidence strongly supports unauthorized activity.

Blocking a card or escalating a case requires human approval before the action is executed.
